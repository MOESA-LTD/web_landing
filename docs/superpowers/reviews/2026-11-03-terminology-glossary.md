# Terminology glossary: TR / DE / BG

Date: 2026-11-03 · Branch: `feat/i18n-terminology` (from `660a749`) · Owner decision: where a sector term has no
precise, natural equivalent, or the native rendering reads as forced or as a calque to a B2B buyer, use the English
industry term; keep a native term where professionals in that market use it.

Legend: **EN** = English term kept · native = native term kept/chosen. Terms the brief listed that the site does not
use (Fulfilment, Due Diligence, MOQ, RFQ, Lead Time, Incoterms, KPI, QC) are not in the copy and are not added.

## Conventions per language

- **TR.** Our translations follow the loanwords of the approved package TR (sourcing, supplier, PO, replenishment,
  trading, listing, brief, marketplace). Suffixes take the curly apostrophe used site-wide: *sourcing’i*,
  *supplier’ı / supplier’a / supplier’dan / supplier’lar*. **Section rule:** where the approved `condensed` line of a
  section uses a Turkish word for the concept, the list item below it uses the same word (e.g. "Tedarikçi
  değerlendirmesi" under "…; tedarikçi değerlendirmesi; …"), so the two lines never clash. Approved package text and
  the service names it defines are unchanged.
- **DE.** English terms are capitalised nouns; compounds are hyphenated (*Private-Label-Entwicklung*,
  *Sourcing-Projekt*, *Digital-Commerce-Operations*). Native terms stay where German procurement and logistics use
  them.
- **BG.** English terms without an established Bulgarian form are written in Latin script, without article or
  inflection, and are built in with prepositions (*проект за sourcing*, *международен sourcing на марки*). Loanwords
  already assimilated in Cyrillic stay Cyrillic (*маркетплейс*, *фасилити мениджмънт*, *лого*).

## Other notable fixes

- **TR:** "Ticaret (Trading) Düzenlemesi" → "Trading Düzeni" (approved wording, no doubled term); "Stok yönetimi ve
  stok yenileme" → "Stok ve replenishment" (5 places); "Satın Alma Siparişi" flow step → "PO".
- **DE:** "Handelsvereinbarung" was used for both "commercial agreement" and "trading arrangement" →
  "Auch ein Geschäftsabschluss muss operativ umgesetzt werden."; "Handelspartner" was used for "retail partner" too →
  "Einzelhandelspartner".
- **BG:** "Commerce operations" was translated as "Търговски операции" (= commercial operations); "търговско
  споразумение" was reused for "commercial agreement" → "И договорената сделка трябва да бъде изпълнена оперативно.";
  "QA" was rendered as quality control; "търговски вериги, вериги магазини" (doubled) → "групи в търговията на
  дребно, вериги магазини"; "Действия от доставчика" made parallel with "Необходими действия от клиента";
  "Разгледайте още за MOESA." → "Научете повече за MOESA."; who-we-work-with meta description shortened to 155
  characters.
- **Legal pages:** no sector terms in the TR/DE/BG legal text; nothing changed there (UNREVIEWED_LEGAL.md unchanged).
- **Slugs:** BG was not live yet, so the two BG service slugs follow the new names without a redirect:
  `/bg/uslugi/sourcing-i-upravlenie-na-snabdyavaneto` (was `sorsing-…`) and
  `/bg/uslugi/digitalna-targoviya-i-commercial-intelligence` (was `…-biznes-analizi`). DE
  `marktzugang-und-handelsvertretung` is unchanged (known, DE-S3; DE is live-gated, rename separately if wanted).
  TR slugs still match.
- **Owner decisions (2026-11-03):** keep the TR section rule (lists follow the word of the approved line above them);
  keep "Trading Partneri" (how-we-work, matches its approved line) and the approved "Ticaret Partneri" (home); keep BG
  "собствени марки"; keep DE "Customer Insights".
