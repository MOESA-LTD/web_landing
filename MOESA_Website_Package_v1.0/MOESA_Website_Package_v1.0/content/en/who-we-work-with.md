# Different business models. One coordinated operating partner.

MOESA works with companies that need products, suppliers, procurement capacity, private-label development, market access or continuing commercial operations.

## Start with the business need, not a predefined package.

A retail group, manufacturer, distributor and digital commerce business may require different commercial structures. MOESA begins with the client’s objective and structures the appropriate operating model around it.


## Retail Groups & Corporate Buyers

For retail groups, chain-store organisations, hospitality companies, healthcare and care organisations, facility-management companies, industrial buyers, service groups and procurement departments.

- Category and assortment development
- Private-label portfolios
- Managed procurement
- Multi-supplier coordination
- Continuity of supply
- International logistics and delivery

## Wholesalers & Distributors

For national or regional distributors, category specialists, importers, B2B suppliers, cash-and-carry operators and multi-brand portfolio businesses.

- International brand sourcing
- Portfolio expansion
- Commercial and territory agreements
- Exclusive or own-brand products
- Purchasing and logistics
- Inventory and replenishment

## Brands & Brand Owners

For established brands, new brand owners, private-label businesses, marketplace-native brands, D2C brands and corporate-owned product brands.

- New product and category development
- Product-family architecture
- Supplier and manufacturer coordination
- Packaging and positioning
- Commerce operations
- Customer-feedback and portfolio improvement

## Manufacturers

For finished-goods manufacturers, OEM and private-label producers, component suppliers and export-focused manufacturers seeking European buyers or operations.

- European market entry
- Distributor and retailer development
- Commercial representation
- Market-specific portfolio preparation
- Import, warehousing and local operations

## Digital Commerce Businesses

For Amazon operators, multi-marketplace businesses, D2C brands, online retailers, omnichannel companies and cross-border e-commerce operators.

- Product sourcing and private label
- Listing and catalog operations
- Marketplace and D2C management
- Inventory and replenishment
- Performance and customer intelligence

## Buyers seeking international brands

A client may already know the brand or product it wants to buy. MOESA identifies the relevant commercial party, initiates contact and coordinates sourcing, negotiation and purchasing.


## Businesses without a full internal procurement team

MOESA can operate as an external procurement and product-operations function within a defined mandate while the client retains strategy, budget and approvals.


## Engagement selection guide

- Need a specific brand or supplier → Sourcing & Managed Procurement
- Need a product or category under your own brand → Product & Private Label Development
- Need European buyers or local representation → Market Access & Commercial Representation
- Need production, logistics, inventory or delivery → Operations & Supply Chain
- Need marketplace, D2C, catalog or performance management → Digital Commerce & Commercial Intelligence
- Need several connected functions → Integrated Operations Partnership

## Not sure yet?

Describe the business problem, current position and desired result. MOESA can help identify the correct starting point without forcing the project into an unnecessary service package.
