# Operate the product. Understand the market. Improve the business.

MOESA supports ongoing commercial operations across marketplaces, direct-to-consumer websites and other digital sales channels, connecting product presentation, catalog, inventory, performance and customer intelligence.

## The work does not end when the product goes live.

Product information must remain accurate. Listings and catalogs must be maintained. Inventory must reflect sales demand. Pricing, promotions, customer feedback and channel performance must be reviewed.


## More than an e-commerce agency.

Digital commerce is one capability within MOESA’s broader product, procurement and commercial operations model. It can operate alone or connect with product development, sourcing, purchasing, supply chain, inventory and replenishment.

The client retains account ownership, brand strategy, budgets, pricing and final risk decisions. MOESA manages the agreed recurring commercial operation and cross-functional follow-up.
