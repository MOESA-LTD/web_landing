# Connecting products, brands and markets.

MOESA supports manufacturers, brand owners, buyers and commercial partners that want to establish international relationships, enter European markets or develop new sales channels.

## A new market requires more than a new customer.

Entering a new market requires the right commercial relationships, product preparation, channel strategy and operational structure. MOESA acts as a local commercial and operating partner within an agreed mandate.


## Two sides of international trade

- For manufacturers and brand owners entering or expanding in Europe
- For retailers, distributors and buyers seeking international brands, manufacturers or suppliers

## Typical mandates

- Enter the European market
- Find a distributor or retail partner
- Establish commercial representation
- Source an international brand for a market
- Build a new retail, wholesale or digital sales channel

## What MOESA manages

- Market assessment
- Product and portfolio preparation
- Commercial positioning
- Partner and channel identification
- Commercial outreach
- Negotiation support
- Local operational preparation
- Ongoing commercial coordination

## Representation structures

- Project-Based Market Entry
- Ongoing Commercial Representation
- Trading and Distribution Arrangement
- Local Operations Partner

## European operating base

MOESA Ltd. is based in Plovdiv, Bulgaria. The exact scope and responsibilities are defined separately for each market, product and commercial relationship.


## Clear mandate and control

The client retains brand ownership, strategy, pricing authority, contractual approvals, partner approval and final commercial decisions. MOESA coordinates the agreed market-development and commercial activities.
