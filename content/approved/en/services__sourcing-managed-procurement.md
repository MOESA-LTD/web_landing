# From sourcing mandate to managed procurement.

Whether you are looking for a specific international brand, a new supplier or an external team to manage recurring purchasing operations, MOESA turns the requirement into an executable procurement process.

## You define what the business needs. We manage how it gets sourced.

A sourcing project may begin with a specific brand, a known product, a target category or a commercial objective. The client retains strategic decisions and approvals; MOESA manages research, communication, negotiation and operational follow-through.


## Typical mandates

- Source a specific international brand
- Find a product or manufacturer
- Replace or diversify a supplier
- Outsource recurring procurement operations
- Coordinate purchasing across multiple suppliers

## What MOESA manages

- Sourcing brief and requirement structure
- Brand, product, manufacturer and supplier research
- Supplier assessment
- Commercial negotiation
- Samples and commercial feasibility
- Purchase-order and supplier coordination
- Supply-chain handover

## Managed procurement model

MOESA can operate as an external procurement function while the client retains strategy, budget authority, final supplier approval and final purchasing approval.

- Ongoing supplier communication
- Purchase-order follow-up
- Pricing and term coordination
- Production-status monitoring
- Documentation follow-up
- Reorder and continuity planning

## Engagement models

- Project-Based Sourcing
- Managed Procurement Service
- Trading Arrangement
- Commercial Representation

## Suitable for

- Retail groups and corporate buyers
- Wholesalers and distributors
- Brands and manufacturers
- Digital commerce businesses

## Clear responsibilities

Final business decisions, supplier approvals and purchasing authority remain with the client unless a separate trading arrangement has been agreed. Where technical or regulatory requirements apply, MOESA coordinates the workflow with the relevant parties.
